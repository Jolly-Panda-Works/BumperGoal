Source: user-supplied Low Poly Tiny Cartoon Racing Cars Asset Pack v1.0.unitypackage.

Nine FBX cars, their original BaseColor atlases, materials and prefabs are retained here. Runtime GLBs are generated with tools/export-tiny-racing-cars.py. No package scripts are executed. Cars are grounded and normalized to a 1.42-unit length; wheel/axle meshes are named for animation.
